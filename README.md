# Limber

Daily stretching and mobility for runners and cyclists. It installs on an iPhone Home Screen and works offline.

- Check in with what you did today (run, ride, both or rest) and where you're tight; Limber builds a 5, 10 or 15 minute session
- Three session types: daily, after a workout, and a slow bedtime wind-down with a dim night screen
- A voice names each stretch, says when to switch sides, and gives you 5 seconds to switch
- 37 stretches with cues, including foam roller and strap work, plus a breathing finisher
- Logs how tight you feel before and after; shows a day streak, a calendar and where your time goes
- Backup and restore to a file; CSV export

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the site in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Limber from your Home Screen and use it there. The installed app keeps its own log.

## Your data

Everything is stored on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Settings → Back up now** from time to time.

## Updating

After changing any file, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`. The app shows an **Update** button the next time it opens.
